HOW TO PLAY

  1. Pull — press & drag back from your seed.
  2. Aim — the arrow points where the seed launches.
  3. Release — let go, seed blasts forward.
  4. Hit the opponent's seed to score +1.
  5. Keep blasting — on a hit you shoot again.
  6. Miss — turn passes to the other player.
  7. First to 25 hits wins.
  8. Topie (T): before you shoot, tap Topie to stand your opponent's seed up on its edge. It's taller and easier to hit for that shot. 3 per game, one per turn.
  9. Online — no sign-up. Tap Play Online to meet a random player, or Play a Friend to send a room link. Online play runs on Rolex 🌯: 24 hours each, 2,000 UGX by Mobile Money in Uganda or $1.99 by card elsewhere. Your friend joins free.
