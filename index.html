THE BLASTING GAME
A childhood game from Uganda.
Local 2-player • First to 25 wins

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Pull — press & drag back from your seed.
  2. Aim — the arrow points where the seed launches.
  3. Release — let go, seed blasts forward.
  4. Hit the opponent's seed to score +1.
  5. Keep blasting — on a hit you shoot again.
  6. Miss — turn passes to the other player.
  7. First to 25 hits wins.
